# Support policy

Commercial support is a product feature, not an informal promise.

## Proposed service levels

- Community: public documentation and issue tracker, best effort.
- Licensed: installation assistance, versioned updates, and private tickets.
- Managed: scheduled migration help and operational review under a separate agreement.

Security reports are acknowledged within one business day. License-blocking installation defects target an initial response within one business day; ordinary defects within two. Custom scripts, leaked assets, unsupported forks, and third-party hosting incidents are outside the standard SLA.

Every release publishes supported FXServer artifact, database version, dependency lock, migration notes, rollback notes, and end-of-support date. Support promises only begin after pilot capacity has been measured.
